Pavlos Aimoniotis, PhD

Pavlos Aimoniotis is a Platform Application Engineer at Intel working on computer architecture and system performance. His work includes CPU microarchitecture, performance analysis, and optimization of real-world workloads, including telecommunications and AI/ML applications.

He received his PhD in Computer Science from Uppsala University. His doctoral research addressed speculative execution, side-channel attacks and defenses, caches, prefetching, and memory protection. He previously studied Electrical and Computer Engineering at the University of Thessaly.
